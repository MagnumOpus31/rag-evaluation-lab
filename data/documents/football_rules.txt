Football Rules

Football is a team sport played between two teams of eleven players. The objective is to score more goals than the opposing team.

A goal is scored when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and underneath the crossbar, provided no offence has been committed by the attacking team.

The offside rule prevents an attacking player from gaining an unfair advantage by being too far forward when the ball is played to them. A player is in an offside position if they are nearer to the opponents' goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent when the ball is played to them, subject to the specific conditions of the Laws of the Game.

A penalty kick is awarded when a player commits a direct free kick offence inside their own penalty area. The penalty kick is taken from the penalty mark.

A yellow card is a caution issued to a player for certain offences or misconduct. A red card sends a player off and means they cannot continue playing in that match.

A corner kick is awarded when the ball completely crosses the goal line after last touching a defending player, without a goal being scored.

A throw-in is awarded to the opposing team when the whole ball crosses the touchline. The throw-in is taken from the point where the ball crossed the line.

The goalkeeper is the player who is primarily responsible for defending the team's goal. Within their own penalty area, the goalkeeper has special privileges under the Laws of the Game, including being permitted to handle the ball subject to those laws.
